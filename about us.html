About Peak Carpenters

Started by a family who loves woodworking, Peak Carpenters has grown from small repairs to full custom projects. We blend traditional skills with modern techniques to deliver reliable results.

We value clear communication, honest pricing, and workmanship you can trust. Our team trains on real projects and treats every job with care—big or small.

We enjoy making polished furniture, sturdy shelves, and custom cabinets that fit your home or workplace.

Peak Carpenters started in Woodlands, Durban, as a small family business making wardrobes, benches, and simple chairs. Today we still focus on polished furniture and strong finishes.

Based in Durban, we serve local homes and businesses and offer training programs for new carpenters.
