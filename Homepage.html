Welcome to Peak Carpenters

We build beautiful, long-lasting woodwork for homes and businesses. From custom furniture to full renovations, our work blends practical skills with thoughtful design.

With over 20 years in the trade, our team focuses on clear communication, smart solutions, and careful craftsmanship—so your project looks great and lasts.

We also enjoy repairs and restorations, bringing old woodwork back to life with neat detail and solid repairs.

Every piece we make is designed to fit your space and your life. We use polished finishes and strong joinery so your furniture lasts for years.

Ready to get started? Contact us for a quick estimate or a friendly chat about your idea.

Based in Durban, we serve local homes and businesses with custom carpentry and training programs for new carpenters.
